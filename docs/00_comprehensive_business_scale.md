## 📋 **POLICY**

### Business Strategy Policies

- **Customer-Centric Development**: All product decisions driven by customer feedback and market needs
- **Greek-First Approach**: Every feature must work perfectly for Greek business requirements
- **Quality Over Speed**: Never compromise product quality for faster releases
- **Transparent Communication**: Open, honest communication with customers, partners, and stakeholders
- **Sustainable Growth**: Build for long-term success, not short-term gains

### Financial Policies

- **Revenue Diversification**: Multiple revenue streams (subscriptions, services, partnerships)
- **Cash Flow Priority**: Maintain 12+ months operating expenses in cash
- **Investment Strategy**: Reinvest 60% of profits into R&D and market expansion
- **Pricing Strategy**: Premium pricing reflecting superior value and Greek expertise
- **Cost Control**: Lean operations with strategic outsourcing where beneficial

### Operational Policies

- **Automation First**: Automate all repeatable processes for efficiency
- **Documentation Standards**: Comprehensive documentation for all business processes
- **Security Excellence**: Enterprise-grade security and compliance standards
- **Scalability Planning**: Build systems that scale to 100,000+ users
- **Continuous Improvement**: Monthly process review and optimization

### Marketing Policies

- **Education-Based Marketing**: Teach Greek businesses about digital transformation
- **Thought Leadership**: Establish expertise through content and speaking engagements
- **Word-of-Mouth Focus**: Prioritize customer satisfaction for organic growth
- **Digital-First Strategy**: Leverage digital channels for cost-effective customer acquisition
- **Partnership-Driven Growth**: Strategic partnerships for market penetration

### Customer Success Policies

- **Proactive Support**: Anticipate and solve customer issues before they arise
- **Success Metrics**: Track customer business outcomes, not just product usage
- **Continuous Training**: Ongoing education to maximize customer value
- **Feedback Integration**: Systematic collection and integration of customer feedback
- **Retention Focus**: Prioritize customer retention over new acquisition
