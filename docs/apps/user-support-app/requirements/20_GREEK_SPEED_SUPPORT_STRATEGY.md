## 🚀 The 4-Layer Support Architecture

### **Layer 1: AI-Powered Instant Support (80% of issues)**

**Greek Business AI Assistant**

- 24/7 instant responses in fluent Greek
- Trained on Greek business law (AADE, IKA, EFKA)
- Customer-specific context and history
- Visual guidance with screenshots
- Voice notes in Greek for complex explanations

**Response Time**: **Instant (< 5 seconds)**

### **Layer 2: Smart Knowledge Base (15% of issues)**

**Industry-Specific Greek Business Guides**

- Restaurant/Food Service procedures
- Shipping/Maritime operations
- Tourism/Hospitality management
- Retail & general business workflows

**Response Time**: **< 30 seconds**

### **Layer 3: Live Chat with Greek Business Experts (4% of issues)**

**Expert Support Team:**

- 5 Greek Business Experts (accounting, HR, industry specialists)
- 3 Technical Support Engineers
- 2 Senior Escalation Specialists

**Availability**: 08:00-22:00 Greek time
**Response Time**: **< 2 minutes**

### **Layer 4: Developer Direct Access (1% of issues)**

**Senior Developer Escalation**

- Immediate escalation for critical system issues
- Screen sharing with live debugging
- Hotfix deployment within hours

**Response Time**: **< 15 minutes for critical issues**
